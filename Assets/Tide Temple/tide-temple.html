The Tide Temple

Flooded ruins. Water level is the lever — raise it and something opens above, lower it and something is exposed below, always with a cost. Spine-with-pockets structure: one linear critical path, optional dead-end pockets branch off and reconnect to the same point.

Collectible rule (applies across all temples): every temple contains at least one collectible that requires holding a specific non-home stone that has not yet been surrendered anywhere — no fallback, no baseline solution. A player who wants full completion must plan their stone-return order and visit temples for these pickups before giving up the required stone at another temple. This is intentional and permanently missable if ignored.

01 The Flooded Nave
Intro · Onboarding
Player enters at a high, dry entryway overlooking a fully flooded chamber. A single floating platform bobs below. First taste of the water-level lever: a shallow basin control that raises the whole room's water a fixed amount, just enough to reach the exit ledge.
THome Raise water once to lift the floating platform to exit-ledge height. Single clean action — no fail state, pure teaching moment.
Pocket Slot · Off the Nave Optional — placement TBD
Earliest possible branch point — good candidate for the no-fallback collectible per the front-loading strategy discussed (reward players who haven't surrendered other stones yet). Short dead-end, reconnects at the same doorway.
Candidate: no-fallback slot
02 The Sunken Vault
Core Puzzle · Lower
Introduces the opposite verb. A submerged doorway is visible underwater through a murky gate. Lowering the water reveals the door — but also exposes a coral turret embedded in the vault wall that was previously silenced.
THome Lower water to reveal the sunken door. Turret opens fire — cross quickly, or raise/lower to silence-and-reopen in bursts.
Coral Turret
03 The Rusted Gate Hall
Core Puzzle · Transform
A long-sealed iron gate blocks the main path. Raising the water fully submerges the gate; holding it there lets it soak. Lowering the water again leaves the gate visibly rusted and weakened. First puzzle where raise/lower is a two-step sequence rather than a single action.
THome Raise to soak the gate, then lower — gate rusts open/weak enough to break through.
Baseline Side corridor with a longer, harder platforming detour around the gate.
Pocket Slot · Off the Gate Hall Optional — placement TBD
Still early enough to fit the "visit before losing stones" front-loading idea. Good spot for a standard fallback-having loot pocket (stone-optional access pattern) rather than the no-fallback one — variety in pocket difficulty/type across the temple.
04 The Switch Cistern
Core Puzzle · Stone-Optional Access
A tall vertical shaft with a switch high on the far wall. No Tide-based route up — deliberately. This room tests whatever else the player is carrying.
NBonus Grow vines up the shaft wall to climb directly to the switch.
IBonus Freeze standing water into stepped platforms up the shaft.
Fallback No bonus stone: narrow crumbling ledges requiring precise platforming, or an endurance fight against a crab brute guarding a slower manual route.
05 The Tide Pool Gauntlet
Puzzle–Combat Hybrid
The signature room. A long gallery, water at mid-level. Lowering water exposes a floor path and wakes a coral turret; raising water opens a higher walkway and brings the player into eel lunge range. Neither state is safe — the player must alternate, using each state only as long as needed.
THome Actively manage raise/lower to thread between turret fire (low water) and eel lunges (high water) while crossing the gallery.
IBonus Freeze an eel mid-lunge to neutralize it — opens a safer window to stay in raised water longer.
Coral Turret Eel / Lamprey
06 The Drowned Choir
Combat
A wide chamber, moderate water level. Drowned corpses shamble in from side alcoves; a siren perches on a high ledge, her charm-pull dragging the player toward open water where the corpses can close in. Straightforward arena fight, but Tide gives real tools rather than being a spectator mechanic.
THome Raise water to force corpses into deeper water where they slow down; trigger a current to shove grouped enemies into the chamber's spike hazard, or to break the siren's line of sight/pull.
Baseline Standard melee/ranged combat against the group without using water at all — Tide tools are an advantage, not a requirement. Resisting/escaping the charm-pull is a timing/input challenge on its own.
Drowned Corpse ×3 Siren
Pocket Slot · Off the Choir Optional — placement TBD
Midpoint pocket. Past the "safe to assume nothing's been lost yet" front-loaded zone, so this is a better home for a standard fallback loot pocket than the no-fallback collectible — a player attempting 100% wouldn't need to rush here specifically before losing stones elsewhere.
07 The Chest Below
Core Puzzle · Lower / Reveal
Small antechamber before the climax. Lowering the water reveals a chest sitting on the drained floor, containing a key item needed for the final door ahead.
THome Lower water fully to expose and open the chest.
08 The Reliquary Depths
Climax Set-Piece
The temple's biggest room — vertical and wide, water level shifting across multiple zones at once rather than one uniform basin. Combines everything taught so far: turret + eel pairing, a rusted-gate echo, a switch-access gap, and a drowned-corpse push — all live simultaneously. The ghost ship / phantom crew makes its appearance here as the temple's unique narrative-flavor threat: spectral sailors that phase in from the deep water as the fight escalates, tying the room back to the "returning what the sea is owed" pirate framing.
THome Full mastery check — manage multiple water zones under time and combat pressure using everything taught in rooms 1–6.
Bonus Any Ice/Nature/Ember held opens a shortcut through one specific zone of the room, reducing exposure time.
Coral Turret Eel / Lamprey Drowned Corpse Ghost Ship Crew
09 The Return
Stone Deposit
Calm, still water — the first fully placid pool in the temple, a deliberate tonal contrast to everything before it. Minimal mechanically: the narrative beat of surrendering Tide. No combat, no puzzle. Let the room breathe.

Structure: 9 spine rooms + 3 optional pocket slots (placement/content still yours to lock). Spine is a strict single line — no forks that reconverge. Pockets are dead-ends that reconnect to the same point on the spine they branch from.

Enemies in this pass: Drowned Corpse, Coral Turret, Eel/Lamprey, Crab Brute, Siren, Ghost Ship Crew. Water Elemental removed per direction.

Collectible system: pocket slots can hold either (a) a standard fallback-having loot pocket, stone-optional access pattern, gettable anytime, or (b) the temple's one true no-fallback collectible requiring an un-surrendered non-home stone. Slot A (earliest) is flagged as the natural candidate for the no-fallback collectible, consistent with front-loading it near the entrance.
